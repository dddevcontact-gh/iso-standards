Term

Quality requirements and evaluation

Relevant standards:

  • 25030 (quality requirements framework)
  • 25040 (evaluation process)
  • 25041 onwards also includes guides for applying 25040


Quality requirements

Quality requirements refer to the requirements each quality measure needs to reach. The standard 25030 describes that quality requirements should be specified using the quality models, not invented otherwise. This comes with the advantage of being clearer and easier to add new employees (especially for those with experience in SQuaRE products). It also helps identify stakeholders and their quality needs, and turn those into requirements. These are preferably based on measures from 2502 - requirements are more verifiable if they have both measures and targets.



Quality evaluation

This refers to the process of evaluating, not evaluating a particular quality. 25040 describes the establishment of the evaluation requirements - specifying the evaluation (its measures, and the criteria), designing it, executing it, and concluding with results. Standards of 25041 onwards apply this process to several perspectives. For example, developers care about quality during development, whereas acquirers care about whether the product meets their needs.
