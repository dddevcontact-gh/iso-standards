Key Terms

Attribute

Inherent property or characteristic of an entity that can be distinguished quantitatively or qualitatively by human or automated means

Component

Entity with discrete structure, such as an assembly or software module, within a system considered at a particular level of analysis

Context of Use

Users, tasks, equipment (hardware, software and materials), and the physical and social environments in which a product is used

Data Quality

Capability of the characteristics of data to satisfy stated and implied needs when used under specified conditions

Developer

Individual or organization that performs development activities (including requirements analysis, design, testing through acceptance) during the system or software life cycle process

Direct User

Person who interacts with the product

Evaluator

Individual or organization that performs an evaluation

ICT Product

Product which uses Information and Communication Technologies (ICTs) and can be part of information system

Indirect User

Person who receives output from a system but does not interact with the system

Information System

System that comprises software, hardware, communication facility, data, and the people who use it in a given (user and system) environment to satisfy their information processing needs (goals)

IT Service

Service that makes use of IT systems as tools to provide value to an individual user or a business by facilitating results the user of business wants to achieve

IT Service System

System that is comprised of an IT Service and the people who use it in a given (user) environment to satisfy their service needs

IT Service Quality

Capability of an IT service to satisfy stated and implied quality needs when delivered under specified conditions

Maintainer

Individual or organisation that performs maintenance activities

Product

Artefact that is produced, is quantifiable, and is deliverable to user as either an end item in itself or a component item

Product Quality

Capability of a system or its components to satisfy stated and implied quality needs when used under specific conditions

Quality Characteristics

Category of quality attributes that bears on the quality of the ICT product or information system

Quality-In-Use

Extent to which the system or product, when it is used in a specified context or use satisfies or exceeds stakeholder’s needs to achieve specified beneficial goals or outcomes

Quality Measure

Derived measure that is defined as a measurement function of two or more values of quality measure elements

Quality Measure Element

Measure defined in terms of a property and the measurement method for quantifying it, including optionally the transformation by a mathematical function

Quality Model

Defined set of characteristics and of relationships between them, which provides a framework for specifying quality requirements and evaluating the quality

Quality Property

Property of a target entity that is related to a quality measure element and which can be quantified by a measurement method

Quality Requirement

Requirement for quality properties or attributes of an ICT product, data, or service that satisfy needs which ensue from the purpose for which that ICT product, data or service is to be used

Quality Sub-Characteristic

Set of one or more quality properties that represent a unique aspect of a quality characteristic

Requirement

Statement which translates or expresses a need and its associated constraints and conditions

Stakeholder

Individual or organisation having a right, share, claim, or interest in a system or in its possession of characteristics that meet their needs and expectations

Stakeholder Need

Prerequesite identified as necessary for a stakeholder, or a set of stakeholders, to achieve an intended outcome, implied or stated within a specific context of use

Sub-Sub-Characteristic

Subdivision of a quality sub-characteristic established by the user of a quality model to provide more granular representation of the quality attributes of a target entity

System

Arrangement of parts or elements that together exhibit a stated behaviour or meaning that the individual constituents do not

Target Entity

Fundamental thing of relevance to the user, about which information is kept, and which needs to be measured

User

Individual user that interacts with a system or benefits from a system during its utilization

Validation

Confirmation, through the provision of objective evidence, that the requirements for a specific intended use or application have been fulfilled

Verification

Confirmation, through the provision of objective evidence, that specified requirements have been fulfilled
