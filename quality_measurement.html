Term

Quality measurement

Relevant standards:

  • 25020 (measurement reference model)
  • 25021 (quality measure elements)
  • 25022 (measures for quality-in-use)
  • 25022 (measures for product quality)
  • 25022 (measures for data quality)


The standards in 2502n are focused upon defining quality measurements. This is the way to measure whether a product / system / component keeps up with certain quality requirements.


Standard 25020 is the measurement reference model, and focuses on being a guide to the rest. It explains how quality measures are constructed, and how it relates back to the quality model’s characteristics. It also talks about how to create more measures when the standard ones do not fit the scenario.


Standard 25021 describes quality measure elements. Measures are calculated from simpler quality measure elements (such as counts, times, sizes etc.), and usually used to find a density of them (such as error density for the size of a program). The standard provides a list of elements to count and a consistent way of describing them.


25022 to 25024 are the same pattern for three different quality models (defined in 2501n):

  • 25022 talks about the measures for the quality-in-use model (effectiveness, efficiency, satisfaction etc.)
  • 25023 talks about the product quality model - so its measures are about properties of the system or software itself
  • 25024 pairs with the data quality model, so its measures are about the data
